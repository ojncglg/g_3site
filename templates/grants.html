Frequently asked questions

Straight answers to the most common grant planning and funding questions.

Can grant funds cover police software and training?

In many programs, yes. Depending on the grant, funds can support software, implementation, training, and modernization initiatives.

Do you guarantee grant awards?

No. We do not guarantee awards. We help you present a strong, compliance-aligned proposal with clear operational impact.

Can we apply for one module first?

Yes. Many agencies start with one module, prove impact, and then expand as funding allows.

What does the grant partner handle?

They help identify fit programs, shape the narrative, and support proposal documentation and submission readiness.

How long does the process usually take?

It depends on program windows and agency readiness, but clear scope and planning typically shorten the timeline.

Can we still move forward if a grant is not awarded?

Yes. We can phase deployment by prioritizing one workflow first and scaling from there.
