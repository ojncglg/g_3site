Overview

The Vacation Book ecosystem gives command one place to manage day‑to‑day staffing with policy guardrails and full accountability. Deploy one module to solve an immediate need—or roll out the full suite for a seamless workflow across scheduling, extra duty, towing, bidding, and forecasting.

  • Scheduling & Calendar (vacation/sick/comp with approvals)
