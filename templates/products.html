Overview

The Vacation Book ecosystem gives command one place to manage day‑to‑day staffing with policy guardrails and full accountability. Deploy one module to solve an immediate need—or roll out the full suite for a seamless workflow across scheduling, extra duty, towing, bidding, and forecasting.

What’s included

  • Scheduling & Calendar (vacation/sick/comp with approvals)
  • Extra Duty (payjobs) intake, vetting, fair assignment, audit
  • Tow Logs with search, custody history, exports
  • Vacation Bidding (seniority/lottery windows, policy caps)
  • Roster Management (units/squads/shifts, least‑privilege access)
  • Staffing Predictor (AI) to flag low‑staff windows early

Why the suite works best

Frequently asked questions

What does Vacation Book replace?

It replaces paper requests, text threads, and disconnected calendars with one policy-aligned workflow for requests, approvals, and command visibility.

Can we start with one module and expand later?

Yes. Many agencies start with one module first, then add others as rollout confidence grows.

Will the system match our policy and rank structure?

Yes. Approval flows and guardrails are built to align with your agency’s policy and chain of command.

How fast can an agency deploy?

Timelines vary by scope, but most deployments start with a focused use case and scale from there.

Do command staff get audit-ready reporting?

Yes. Actions are logged in one audit trail and records can be exported for policy reviews and operational reporting.

How much training do officers need?

Training is role-specific and practical, so users focus only on the workflows that matter to their assignment.
