Why extra duty becomes a headache

Extra duty is some of the most complained-about work in policing, and it is almost always a process problem, not a people problem. Jobs get offered to whoever the coordinator knows first. Conflicts get discovered after the fact. Cancellations leave gaps. When officers cannot see how assignments were made, fairness becomes a rumor.

Structured job intake

One request form captures the job details, location, hours, and rate requirements. No more phone calls, texts, and paper slips competing for the coordinator's attention.

Fair, visible assignment

Your assignment rules, rotation order, hours balancing, or eligibility tiers, applied automatically to every job. Every decision is logged, so fairness is documented, not rumored.

Conflict checks before assignment

Jobs are checked against the master schedule and officer commitments before assignment, catching overlapping shifts and rest-period issues in advance instead of on the day of the job.

Audit-ready records

Every job, assignment, cancellation, and payment record lives in one trail that command can review at any time. Accountability without the spreadsheet archaeology.
