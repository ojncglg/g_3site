Packages built around how agencies operate

Essential

Start with the operational foundation

Manage leave requests from submission through approval, denial, or waitlist with role-based access. View coverage through daily rosters, configure units, squads, shifts, officer statuses, and skills, and keep access controls and audit history in one place.

  • Vacation Book
  • Daily rosters, units, squads, shifts, and statuses
  • Pairings, assignments, and coverage calendars
  • Staffing graphs that forecast low-staff windows
  • Least-privilege access and audit history
Operations

Manage staffing and planning

The best fit for agencies that want a stronger planning workflow. Operations includes vacation bidding with custom rules, tie breakers, bidding lanes, selection windows, and defined seasons. It also lets you build training schedules and track officer training requirements in one place.

  • Everything in Essential
  • Vacation Bidding
  • Training Tracker
  • Better command planning
Command Suite

Run the full command workflow

Best for agencies that want full control of staffing, extra-duty work, tips, and tow workflows. Command Suite connects pay jobs and part-time assignments to the broader staffing picture, so agencies know where officers are working and can customize sign-up and assignment rules around their own policies.

  • Everything in Operations
  • Pay Jobs
  • Anonymous Tips
  • Tow Logs

Vacation Book
Leave requests, approvals, calendars, coverage, daily rosters, units, squads, shifts, statuses, pairings, assignments, staffing graphs that forecast low-staff windows, least-privilege access, and audit history.
Daily roster and shift pairings
Gives supervisors a clear view of who is working, where they are assigned, and who they are paired with for the day. Agencies can organize officers by squad, shift, unit, status, and assignment so supervisors can make daily staffing decisions without chasing multiple spreadsheets or paper rosters.
Coverage charts and audit logs
Help command staff see staffing levels across the schedule and identify low-coverage windows before they become operational problems. Audit history keeps a record of key changes, approvals, assignments, and roster updates so agencies can review decisions and maintain accountability.
Vacation Bidding
Supports seniority or lottery-based vacation bidding, including separate bidding lanes, defined bidding seasons, selection windows, policy caps, fair resolution, and automatic calendar placement.
Training Tracker
Supports training day planning, availability checks, roster impact, and assignment visibility while giving officers clear instructions on where to report and helping the training unit track attendance.
Pay Jobs
Pay Jobs helps agencies manage extra-duty and part-time assignments from request through signup, approval, assignment, and payment tracking. Agencies can configure job rules, eligibility, rotation, customer billing, and officer assignment workflows around their own policies while keeping command staff aware of where officers are working.
Anonymous Tips
Anonymous Tips gives agencies an outside-facing page where citizens can submit information without identifying themselves. Each tip is routed to an administrator for review, triage, and dissemination to the appropriate unit or officer, helping agencies keep incoming information organized, accountable, and moving toward the right people.
Tow Logs
Tow companies are assigned unit designators and can submit tow sheets with the required information for private tows and repossessions. The officer's role is simplified to running the vehicle through NCIC and verifying the entry.
