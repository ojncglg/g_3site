The A Squad rides south to Medieval Times Baltimore for a night of feasting, jousting, and questionable chivalry, all in honor of Sir Joseph, who leaves the squad after three years. Huzzah.

The brave souls riding to Baltimore. Portraits courtesy of the court painter; bios are historically accurate-ish.

Known as "The Bitter Steel". Paid her tribute of five patches in full and fears no reckoning.

Sir Joseph the Departing, three winters strong on the A Squad. The realm feasts in his honor before he rides to new lands. All hail, and no take-backs.
