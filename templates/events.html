Sir Joseph's Going-Away Night

The A Squad rides south to Medieval Times Baltimore for a night of feasting, jousting, and questionable chivalry, all in honor of Sir Joseph, who leaves the squad after three years. Huzzah.

Date

Friday, December 18, 2026

Show

7:00 PM, Knight's Banquet package

Castle

Medieval Times Baltimore

7000 Arundel Mills Circle, Hanover, MD

Doors

Open 6:00 PM

60 minutes before showtime. Group checks in under Grigori's name.

The Royal Treasury

$91.14 per person. Group total $1,822.80 for 20 adults, all-inclusive (tax and gratuity included). That is the castle's group rate, the lowest they offer.

Coin due: October 31. The crown only has to put down the 25% deposit (about $456) to lock our seats, but he wants everyone's money by the end of the month. Final headcount and full payment are due to the castle December 4.

21st seat: Grigori's +1 (the Lady Caroline). We have asked the castle whether we can add one more to the party later; awaiting their raven.
