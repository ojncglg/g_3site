What is G3 IMPACT?

Meaning: G3 IMPACT stands for Innovation Module Partnership for Agency- Centered Technology.

Program model: Agencies bring real operational and administrative challenges. When a problem is a strong platform fit with clear workflow value, G3 may partner directly with the agency to design and build a module inside the G3 ecosystem.

This is not generic software development. This is targeted problem-solving built around the real needs of public safety organizations.

Built around real-world agency pain points

Too many administrative problems in public safety become accepted as “the way things are”: paper workflows, duplicated effort, poor visibility, fragmented communication, and manual steps that should have been solved years ago.

G3 IMPACT exists to change that.

The best solutions come from people closest to the friction: officers, supervisors, administrators, and command staff. Through G3 IMPACT, agencies help shape software based on real operations, not guesswork.

Who Should Apply

G3 IMPACT is designed for agencies that have identified a clear administrative or workflow problem and want to help shape a practical solution.

This program may be a strong fit if your agency:

  • Has an internal administrative process that wastes time
  • Relies on manual workarounds, spreadsheets, paper, or fragmented systems
  • Has a recurring workflow issue that creates confusion or delay
  • Wants to improve efficiency without forcing people into clunky software
  • Is willing to collaborate on a real-world solution
  • Wants to help shape technology designed for public safety operations

This program is especially valuable for agencies that have a problem no one has meaningfully solved yet.

What kind of problems are we looking for?

We are looking for real administrative and operational workflow issues such as:

  • State Required Annual Training Tracking
  • Police Activity Report-Time Sheets
  • Inventory Tracking Issues
  • Repetitive Data-Entry Tasks
  • Paper-Based or Email-Based Processes that Should Be Automated
  • Agency-Specific Workflow Problems that Do Not Fit Existing Software

If it slows your officers down, creates confusion, or has become “normal” simply because no one has fixed it, it may be a good fit for G3 IMPACT.

How G3 IMPACT Works

1

Identify the problem

Tell us about an administrative or workflow issue inside your agency. Explain what the problem is, who it affects, and why it matters.

2

Submit your idea

Share the current process, the pain points, and the operational impact. The clearer the problem, the better we can evaluate it.

3

G3 reviews the opportunity

We assess whether the problem is a fit for our platform, whether it has meaningful value for your agency, and whether the resulting solution could help other agencies as well.

4

Agency selection

If the opportunity is a strong fit, your agency may be invited to participate in the G3 IMPACT Program as a design partner.

5

Collaborative development

We work with your agency to understand the workflow, identify the best solution path, and build the module inside the G3 platform.

6

Early access and refinement

Partner agencies receive early involvement and practical collaboration as the solution is refined for real-world use.

What Partner Agencies Receive

Agencies selected for G3 IMPACT may receive:

  • Direct collaboration with G3 Industries on a real agency problem
  • Priority input during module design and workflow development
  • Early access to the resulting functionality
  • The opportunity to shape practical tools built around real operations
  • Recognition as a G3 IMPACT partner, where appropriate
  • A stronger platform aligned with public safety realities

This is an opportunity to help shape a solution that works for your agency while contributing to broader innovation in public safety administration.

How Product Ownership Works

Modules developed through the G3 IMPACT Program are built within the G3 platform. G3 Industries retains ownership of the resulting software, product functionality, and module rights, including the ability to improve, reuse, and offer those capabilities to other agencies.

This model allows us to keep investing in the platform while turning agency-specific challenges into scalable solutions that can benefit the wider public safety community.

Our goal is simple and sincere: help cops solve administrative pain points for other cops. If your agency has a workflow problem, chances are other agencies are facing that same challenge. Let’s fix them together and build long-term value for partner agencies and future customers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does submitting an idea guarantee acceptance into the program?

No. All submissions are reviewed, but only selected opportunities will be invited into the G3 IMPACT Program.

Does the problem have to be unique to our agency?

No. The problem can be agency-specific or common across many agencies. What matters most is whether it is meaningful, operationally relevant, and a strong fit for the platform.

Does G3 keep ownership of the module?

Yes. Modules developed through G3 IMPACT are built inside the G3 platform, and G3 Industries retains ownership of the resulting product and software rights.

Will our agency have input into the solution?

Yes. Selected agencies collaborate with G3 during the design and development process to help ensure the module reflects real-world workflow needs.

Is this custom software development for any agency that asks?

No. G3 IMPACT is a selective design-partner program. Opportunities are evaluated based on fit, operational value, platform alignment, and broader applicability.

Who should submit an idea?

Officers, supervisors, administrators, command staff, and others who understand an agency workflow problem are welcome to submit ideas, subject to their agency’s internal policies and approval process where applicable.

We are looking for real administrative and workflow challenges that create inefficiency, delay, unnecessary manual work, or recurring frustration inside public safety organizations.

If your submission is a strong fit, your agency may be considered for the G3 IMPACT Program.
