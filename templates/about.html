Founder

A law enforcement professional from Delaware with 13 years of broad experience and a degree in Digital Media from Harvard Extension founded G3 Industries to bridge the gap between policing and modern technology. Our focus is delivering tools that leadership trusts and officers actually like using.

  • 13 years of broad law enforcement experience (Delaware)
  • Digital Media, Harvard Extension School
  • Command efficiency with rank‑and‑file in mind
  • Built‑in accountability: approvals, audit logs, exports

“After more than a decade in law enforcement, I’ve seen how agencies adopt major technological advancements but still struggle with simple day-to-day processes. Too often, our core administrative systems get left behind, forcing officers and supervisors to work with outdated tools. My mission with G3 Industries is to close that gap — to take the everyday processes that keep departments running and advance them with technology that is clear, accountable, and built for law enforcement. We don’t chase trends; we modernize what matters.”
