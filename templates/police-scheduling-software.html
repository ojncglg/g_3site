The problem with how most agencies schedule

Schedules live in spreadsheets. Time-off requests arrive by text, email, and paper slips. Extra duty is coordinated in a group chat. Coverage risk gets discovered the morning of the shift, and overtime surprises show up after the fact. Every handoff between disconnected tools is a place where staffing truth gets lost.

One scheduling calendar

Shifts, leave, extra duty commitments, and training in a single view. Command sees who is available and who is committed before the shift starts, not after.

Policy guardrails, not workarounds

Common questions

Does police scheduling software replace our current scheduling process?

It replaces the disconnected parts: spreadsheets, texts, and paper forms become one shared scheduling calendar with approvals and policy guardrails. Your shift structure and policies stay the same.

Can it handle our shift structure?

Yes. Rotations, 8s, 10s, 12s, and mixed squad structures are configured to match how your agency actually staffs, including rank-based rules and minimums.

Does it work for small agencies?

Yes. Small departments feel scheduling friction hardest because one absence hits coverage immediately. Pilot programs start with a single squad or workflow.

How long does a pilot take?

Pilots are scoped in weeks, not months. You start with one high-friction workflow, measure the impact, and expand from there.
